# Mathematical audit checklist

- Define every symbol, dimension, independent variable, observable, and sign convention.
- For every equation record origin, apparatus applicability, term-by-term process and sign, units,
  variable roles, competing balances, dominant/negligible limits, assumptions, and falsifier.
- Translate every arrow in the controlled-input-to-observable chain into equations.
- Reject undefined forcing/coupling functions and generic normalized placeholder predictions.
- Recompute every example table or curve from its cited displayed equation.
- Link every conclusion deduction to an equation, generated prediction, or cited mechanism.
- Show the governing balance, constitutive closure, boundary/initial conditions, and geometry.
- State every approximation with its scale separation or nondimensional criterion.
- Check units term by term and recover known limits.
- Separate fitted, measured, estimated, and literature parameters.
- Map each causal-chain link to the equation or mark it explicitly unresolved.
- Record whether the candidate predicts the prompt's primary observable and over what regime.
- Identify sensitivity, identifiability, degeneracy, and validation data requirements.
- Reject or downgrade a model if its simplification deletes the proposed physical essence.
- Block every controlled parameter absent from an equation, term, boundary, or initial condition.
- Block every state/history variable without an evolution or closure equation.
- Verify exact source locators against retrieved full text; record unsuccessful locator searches.
- Require a complete source-to-report transformation map for every adapted equation.
- Distinguish numerical verification from physical validation against independent measurements.

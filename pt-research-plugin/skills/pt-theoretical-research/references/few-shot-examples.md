# Few-shot structural examples

These examples define reasoning shape, not scientific truth. Re-search and verify every source, equation, regime, and selection.

## Pinhole sunglasses

Preserve two prompt objectives: reduce intensity and help correct myopia. A provisional essence may describe an aperture array changing the effective pupil: geometric defocus decreases with admitted angular bundle, diffraction grows for small holes, and open area/overlap controls throughput.

Represent two linked branches:

```text
array geometry → pupil transmission → defocused propagation → retinal PSF/MTF → correction metric
array geometry + pupil overlap → optical throughput → intensity metric
```

Consider a Fourier-optics pupil-mask whole-chain candidate, a geometric-defocus plus diffraction scaling baseline, and an open-area throughput component. Explicitly consider and, when evidence supports it, filter thin-lens-only, pure geometric pinhole-only, Beer-Lambert-through-opaque-film, product-comfort-as-core-objective, and OpenFOAM-as-model candidates. Do not invent scalar weights; allow a Pareto-front outcome.

## Singing capacitor

Branch between a PCB-mounted multilayer ceramic capacitor and a free/lightly supported ceramic body.

```text
voltage waveform → dielectric electric field → electromechanical strain/nonlinear response
→ equivalent force or displacement → ceramic/board modes → surface velocity
→ acoustic radiation → sound-pressure spectrum
```

Require a constitutive relation, structural response model, and acoustic mapping or explicit unresolved gap. Preserve harmonic and DC-bias predictions. Consider board-mediated and direct-radiator chains plus electrostatic/Maxwell-stress alternatives. Explicitly consider incomplete RLC-only, unspecified-force PCB-only, universally linear piezoelectric, thermoelastic-primary, and corona/arcing candidates, recording residual uses and reconsideration conditions.
